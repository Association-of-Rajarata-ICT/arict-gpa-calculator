**Full description** (Markdown):

```markdown
## Your GPA, semester by semester

The **ARICT GPA Calculator** is a free web app for students of the Faculty of Applied Sciences, Rajarata University of Sri Lanka. Choose your department and whether you are doing a BSc (3 years) or BSc Honours (4 years), and every course loads with its credits and GPA weight. Add the grades you have, and your semester, cumulative and overall GPA update as you go, together with the degree class you are on track for. The next-class planner then shows exactly what it takes to move up.

There is no sign-in and no server. Grades are saved in your own browser, and nothing is sent anywhere.

## Key features

### All five departments
Biological Sciences, Chemical Sciences, Computing, Health Promotion and Physical Sciences. Students with more than one subject can turn subjects on or off, move a course to the other semester, hide courses they did not take, or add their own.

### BSc or BSc Honours
Pick your degree right after your department. A BSc counts Years 1 to 3; BSc Honours counts all four. For a BSc, Year 4 is marked *Honours only* and its grades are left out of your GPA.

### Next-class planner (new)
On a Pass and aiming for Second Lower? On Second Lower and want Second Upper? The planner works it out from the grades you already have:

- **What each class needs:** for First Class, Second Upper, Second Lower and Pass, the average GPA your remaining courses need, or whether that class is already secured or out of reach.
- **A grade to aim for in every remaining course:** most courses get one grade, with a step higher on the courses with the most credits, because those move your GPA most. It never suggests below C.
- **What-if:** change any target grade and watch your projected final GPA and class update, with how far you are above or below your goal.
- **In your PDF too:** the report includes your path to the next class and every target grade.

It works for all five departments. Plans use the GPA cut-offs; the handbook's minimum-grade rules also apply.

### Every GPA in one place
- Semester GPA and cumulative GPA for every year of your degree
- Overall GPA with GPA credits and courses graded
- Degree class: First Class, Second Class (Upper), Second Class (Lower) or Pass, with how far you are from the next class

### A PDF report that is also your backup
Download a designed PDF report with your overall GPA, class, a semester GPA chart with your cumulative GPA and the degree-class zones, your plan for the next class, and every graded course with its credits and grade points. You can add your name and registration number (for example `ICT/2022/074`), or skip that and download straight away.

The same PDF carries a copy of your grades. If you clear your browser or change phones, choose **Restore from PDF**, pick the report, and every grade comes back.

### Built on the faculty rules
Grade points, credits and class bands follow the Faculty of Applied Sciences Student Handbook 2022/23:

| Rule | How it works |
| --- | --- |
| GPA | Σ (grade point × credits) ÷ Σ credits |
| Credits | The second digit of the course code: ICT 1**4**07 is a 4-credit course |
| Non-GPA courses | FDN courses and IDC 1202 count zero credits |
| Rounding | Truncated to two decimals, not rounded |
| Class bands | First 3.70 · Second Upper 3.30 · Second Lower 3.00 · Pass 2.00 |

### Fast and easy to use
A home page explains what the site does, and the calculator and planner live on their own page. Works on phones and desktops, in dark and light themes, with smooth scrolling, page transitions and an interactive 3D hero. A built-in FAQ and grading guide explain how your GPA is calculated.

## Important

This is an unofficial study aid made by ARICT. Your official results are on the university's Examination Information System (EIS).

**Try it:** [gpa.arict.lk](https://gpa.arict.lk), or go straight to the [calculator](https://gpa.arict.lk/calculator)
```
